# Training Plan: 2026-08

Growth-biased hybrid block: rebuild strength, gain size, maintain aerobic fitness, and reintroduce controlled explosiveness. Size remains primary; secondary running targets: sub-19:00 5K on 2026-08-26; comfortable completion from the regular hybrid routine half marathon on 2026-11-22.

## 2026-08-01 - Easy Aerobic

Category: easy_run
Run volume: 5 km
Macros: 3400 kcal, 165 g protein, 485 g carbs, 95 g fat
Macro timing: 30-60 g carbs 60-120 min before running, then 25-35 g protein plus carbs after.

Adjustments:
- Protective changes from prior-month body-part feedback.
- Reduced for prior-month recovery feedback.

Plan:
- Easy run RPE 3-4: 5 km at conversational effort. Lower-body feedback: use the safest comfortable range and stop if joints or back flare.
- Mobility: 8-10 min for hips, calves, ankles, and thoracic rotation.
- Fueling: use a small carbohydrate snack if training early or after poor sleep.
