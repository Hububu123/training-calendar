# Training Plan: 2026-08

Growth-biased hybrid block: rebuild strength, gain size, maintain aerobic fitness, and reintroduce controlled explosiveness. Size remains primary; secondary running targets: sub-19:00 5K on 2026-08-22; 1:35 half marathon on 2026-11-22.

## 2026-08-01 - Easy Aerobic

Category: easy_run
Run volume: 5 km
Macros: 3400 kcal, 165 g protein, 485 g carbs, 95 g fat
Macro timing: 30-60 g carbs 60-120 min before running, then 25-35 g protein plus carbs after.

Plan:
- Easy run RPE 3-4: 5 km at conversational effort.
- Mobility: 8-10 min for hips, calves, ankles, and thoracic rotation.
- Fueling: use a small carbohydrate snack if training early or after poor sleep.

## 2026-08-02 - Longer Aerobic

Category: long_run
Run volume: 8 km
Macros: 3600 kcal, 170 g protein, 550 g carbs, 100 g fat
Macro timing: 80-110 g carbs in the 2-3 hours before running, 25-35 g protein after, and another carb-heavy meal within 2 hours.

Plan:
- Easy run RPE 3-4: 8 km conversational; keep the finish controlled.
- Recovery: protein, carbohydrates, fluids, and gentle mobility after the run.

## 2026-08-03 - Upper Strength

Category: gym
Run volume: 0 km
Macros: 3350 kcal, 175 g protein, 455 g carbs, 95 g fat
Macro timing: 35-45 g protein in 4 meals, 40-70 g carbs before lifting, and a protein-plus-carb meal after training.

Plan:
- Warm-up: 5-8 min easy cardio, joint preparation, and ramp sets for the first movement.
- Bench press: 3 working sets at RPE 8.
- Pull-ups: 3 working sets at RPE 8.
- Overhead press or Arnold press: 3 working sets at RPE 8.
- Chest-supported row: 3 working sets at RPE 8.
- Lateral raises: 3 working sets at RPE 8.
- Pallof press: 3 working sets at RPE 7.
- Progression: double progression; add load only after clean top-range reps at RPE 8 or lower, about 2 RIR.

## 2026-08-04 - Lower Strength

Category: gym
Run volume: 0 km
Macros: 3500 kcal, 180 g protein, 505 g carbs, 100 g fat
Macro timing: hit the higher protein target with 40-45 g protein in 4 meals, plus 50-80 g carbs before heavy lifting and carbs again after.

Plan:
- Warm-up: 5-8 min easy cardio, joint preparation, and ramp sets for the first movement.
- Hack squat or goblet squat: 3 working sets at RPE 8.
- Romanian deadlift: 3 working sets at RPE 8.
- Seated or lying hamstring curl: 3 working sets at RPE 8.
- Seated calf raise: 3 working sets at RPE 7.
- Tibialis raises: 4 working sets at RPE 7.
- Side plank: 2 working sets at RPE 7.
- Activation: light good mornings: 2 x 12-15 at RPE 5-6 using no more than 50% of normal working load; stop if symptoms rise or the next morning is worse.
- Progression: double progression; add load only after clean top-range reps at RPE 8 or lower, about 2 RIR.

## 2026-08-05 - Recovery

Category: recovery
Run volume: 0 km
Macros: 3150 kcal, 165 g protein, 385 g carbs, 105 g fat
Macro timing: keep protein evenly split across meals and place most carbs around activity.

Plan:
- Recovery: no working resistance sets, running, sprinting, or plyometrics.
- Optional: easy walking and gentle mobility at RPE 2-3 if comfortable.
